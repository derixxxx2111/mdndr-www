Our product

Zapukaj keeps every knock on the map.

Reps see their territory, every visit and where to go next. Managers see real results calculated from visit history, not self-reported numbers. Live on iOS, Android and the web.

◎

Territory map

Every visited address is a pin coloured by outcome. Managers assign work areas, so nobody knocks on the same door twice.

▦

Land-parcel data

Parcel and infrastructure data from Polish public registers, processed by our own GIS pipeline.

↯

Offline-first

Pins and notes are saved on the device and synced when the network comes back.

⛨

Privacy by design

No GPS tracking of reps. Access is invite-only and approved by a manager.
