Software for sales teams that work in the field.

MDNDR is a Polish company founded in 2023. We build Zapukaj, a mobile and web app for door-to-door sales teams, and run our own sales operations on AI assistants built with Claude.

Our product: Zapukaj

Zapukaj is the field app for door-to-door sales teams. Reps see their territory, every visit and where to go next. Managers see real results calculated from visit history, not self-reported numbers.

zapukaj.pl App Store

Territory map

Every visited address is a pin coloured by outcome. Managers assign work areas, so nobody knocks on the same door twice.

Land-parcel data

Parcel and infrastructure data from Polish public registers and GIS services, processed by our own pipeline into lightweight packages for the app.

Offline-first

Pins and notes are saved on the device and synced when the network comes back. Works on iOS, Android and the web.

Privacy by design

No GPS tracking of reps. Access is invite-only and approved by a manager.

How we build

We are a small team with an AI-first workflow. The Zapukaj app (iOS, Android, web) is developed with Anthropic's Claude Code. Our day-to-day sales operations - lead handling, call preparation, marketing content and reporting - run on Claude-based assistants and automations that we build in-house.
